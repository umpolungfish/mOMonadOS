Circuit: align to_primitive to the Lean, and close both open questions

AminoAcid::to_primitive disagreed with GeneticCode.lean on three amino acids: Tyr
was sent to a Criticality value, putting two acids on ⊙ and leaving < with none,
and His and Asn were transposed. The Lean is built from the primitives and is the
origin of these truths, so it is the arbiter. Tyr now takes a Polarity value, the
phenol OH being a ℤ₂ phospho switch, and His and Asn are put back on Scope and
Composition. `circuit drift` reports agreement.

The Python in red-hot_rebis carries a third assignment, His to ⊙ with an
imidazole-pKa rationale and Gln to Γ. It does not get a vote and is not treated
as correct; it is left untouched here rather than rewritten from a different
notation set.

Both questions the doc left open are closed rather than deferred.

The section choice is free. μ routes through the amino acid, so μ∘δ = id holds
for every codon carrying the glyph, not only the representative δ picks.
section_choice_is_free checks this exhaustively. The section is determined up to
a choice that changes nothing, so the enumeration order is not load-bearing.

The ground layer is silent by construction. codon_role is total: every codon is a
primitive, a scaffold, or a stop, and codon_census reports twenty-three, thirty-
eight and three, summing to sixty-four. strand_word prints scaffold and stop
distinctly instead of collapsing both to one dot. The eight ground-layer acids
are exactly the four-fold degenerate ones and the Lean proves they carry no
primitive, so the silence is the 20 = 8 + 12 theorem rather than a hole.

circuit table gains the degeneracy count per glyph: Met and Trp have one codon,
Ile three, the rest two.
