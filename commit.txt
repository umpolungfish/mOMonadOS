straus cascade: the frontier falling rung by rung

New verb, straus cascade <lo> <hi>. At each rung it asks whether M = n(n+r)/4
carries a divisor w with w = -1 (mod r); the cofactor u = M/w is then the closing
divisor. Over the 1271 frontier values below 200000 the cascade is 873 at rung 3,
277 at 7, 64 at 11, and down to two values that no cofactor of M reaches at any
rung up to 51 — 2521 and 196561, which need a divisor of M^2.

At the greedy rung the test is a prime residue and nothing more, since every
frontier value has M = 1 (mod 3).
