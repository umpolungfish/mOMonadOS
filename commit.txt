The proof vehicle carries the fiducial

The vehicle shipped the field data and left the fiducial out, on the grounds
that the numerical seeds are dead. That reasoning was about the route we went
around, not about the object. The fiducial travels now as the exact extraction:
data/d2048_exact_extraction.txt is the kernel's own `d2048 exact`, carrying the
Stark unit with its minimal polynomial, the two reciprocal embeddings that are
the Galois parts, the monomial by two independent routes, and each radical of
the discriminant recovered from its own Gauss sum. The derivation rides beside
it, along with the Welch overlaps and the verification transcript. The .npz
optimisation output stays out, being a different object.

Transcripts are emitted from the hosted binary at build time and start at the
prompt, so the file and the command a reader types cannot disagree.

The paper carried is alt_sic_moduli_conductor with its four figures, replacing
the older copy. And `d2048 welch` no longer says no vector is recovered at 2048:
it reports what it measures at twelve and names the verb that computes the
moduli at 2048, the phase assignment across the orbit being the object it does
not have.
