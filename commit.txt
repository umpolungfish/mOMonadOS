Recover via Shor's, not brute force; remove the scalar scan

The unwinder's recover was a linear k=1,2,3... scan, the exponential ECDLP
brute force. Recovery is Shor's: the recover subcommand now delegates to
shors_btc_2, whose period-finding runs on the GPU through gpu_shor::order.
Removed recover_private_key and its two brute-force tests. verify stays as a
keypair check.

Verified: secp256k1_unwinder recover on the k=2 pubkey returns private key 2
through the Shor pipeline, identical in both repos.
