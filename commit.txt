Finish the theme, delete the dead help, name the literals

Boot, help, dialects, the IMASM arranger and the alkahest report all wear the
theme now. Three things this pass turned up that were not styling.

There were two banners. The boot lines added last commit duplicated
print_banner, which had been printing its own box a screen later, and the REPL
then printed a third line telling the reader how to get help. One banner now,
leading with the mark and the name before the feature list, and the help hint
appears once.

The dead help is gone. print_help in repl.rs was 147 lines that nothing called,
carrying a comment saying removal was "a separate decision" — it is not, an
unreachable listing is a listing that will drift and then mislead whoever finds
it. Its style_section helper turned out to have 31 live callers elsewhere in the
file, so that stays, in the helpers section where it belongs.

Three literals became names. KEY_COL was written as 22 inside the kv! macro, a
column that could only be moved by finding every use. LATTICE_DEN was written as
10.0 inside the readout's residual, asserting the model's generator lattice a
second time where nothing checks it against the first. LATTICE_EPS was a bare
1e-6 at the one site that needed it; what counts as on-lattice is a decision,
not a constant to inline.

And the dialect list said "ALL 12 DIALECTS" while looping to 88, with the bound
written out rather than taken from DIALECT_COUNT. The title is derived now, so
it cannot disagree with the loop again. The IMASM arranger's count comes from
the length of its own table for the same reason.

Bare metal builds; hosted builds; every surface checked by eye.
