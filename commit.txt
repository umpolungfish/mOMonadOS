Say imscriptive

The kernel described its own Ð=𐑦 primitive, its ⊣ closure and its Protocol B round-trip as
holographic. That is the borrowed word, and the framework has its own: the boundary
imscribes the bulk. Fifteen sites in hand-written kernel prose now say so, including the
one that read "boundary↔bulk imscriptive closure" — which had both words, doing one job.

Two neighbours went with it. `crystal.rs` and `riemann_sic.rs` had the boundary *encoding*
the bulk; imscription is not encoding.

Two uses stay. `constant_closure.rs` cites holographic dark energy, which is the standard
cosmological term and not a description of this framework — renaming it would break the
reference. And `catalog_ask_subset.rs` mirrors the canonical IG_catalog.json, so its
wording belongs to the catalog and has to change there and be regenerated, not be edited
in the copy.
