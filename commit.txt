Drop four module-level allows the crate root already carries

main.rs has #![allow(uncommon_codepoints)] with the reason written above it: the IG
primitives are Shavian glyphs and the glyph is the name, so the lint is answered rather
than obeyed. The same attribute was repeated in triple_frame, hqe, kernel_torus and ovm,
where it does nothing — the lint is crate-level only — and each repetition warned about
being ignored.

Removed from the four modules. The crate-level allow is unchanged and is the one that
does the work. Build is warning-free.
