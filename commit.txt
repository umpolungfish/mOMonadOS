fibqc is documented where the commands are documented.

USER_GUIDE.md lists every REPL command and had no entry for fibqc at all, and
the README described fibonacci_qc.rs without ever naming the command that
reaches it. Both fixed.

The guide entry covers what the two error lines mean, since the split-and-fuse
distinction is not self-explanatory from the output, and records that a flat
`gain: 1.0x` means dictionary-limited rather than converged, which is the
reading the depth-10 T·S row invites and gets wrong. It states the depth ceiling
as a memory fact with the measured figures rather than a vague practical limit,
notes that the arena reports its own high-water mark because exhaustion is
otherwise silent, explains why the word check is a resynthesis rather than the
determinant identity, and warns about the serial console eating the first
character when the REPL is driven by pipe.
