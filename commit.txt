straus budget: which of the two actually fails

The group criterion says -1 must lie in the subgroup the primes of M generate;
the real criterion says a divisor of M^2 must reach it. straus budget separates
them. Below 50000 the gap appears at 19 of 379 rungs walked, and 2521 owns all
but one of those — it fails that way at r = 11, 19, 43, 47, 59, 63, 67, 75, 95
and beyond, while every other value fails because its subgroup lacks -1 outright.
The other case is 1201 at r = 11.
