Evaluate the monomial, and find the form that cannot be read

`d2048 exact` named the S-unit monomial and never computed it. The line that
looked like the product multiplied by the literal 3.0 cubed, not by g3, so
what it printed was not the monomial and was not anything.

Computing it needs room: eps^(-1) g3^3 g4^2 expands to a numerator near 7.4e19
and i64 stops at 9.2e18, so the product now runs in i128, where it fits with
three orders to spare. eps has norm 1, so its inverse is its conjugate and the
whole monomial is multiplication with no division anywhere.

Then the kernel printed -4096, and that is the thing worth keeping. The exact
triple is exact -- it reproduces to the last digit against an independent
bignum -- and it is also the one form of this number that f64 cannot read. Both
terms are 7.35e19, they agree through twenty digits, and the value that
survives is 4.9e-4. The ulp up there is 8192, so the expansion returns half an
ulp of rounding and calls it a number. Each factor on its own is O(1) and loses
nothing, so the monomial is evaluated FACTORED and the expanded triple is kept
as what it is, exact and unevaluable.

Exactness of a representation and evaluability of that representation are two
different properties. This one has the first without the second, and the report
now shows both readings side by side so the difference is on the surface rather
than hidden in a digit.

The monomial comes out 0.000488281016885 against 1/d = 0.00048828125, a gap of
-2.33e-10. Verification surface unchanged: fibqc verify all pass, Frobenius
closed. Both source trees carry the same file.
