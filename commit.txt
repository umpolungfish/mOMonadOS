anyon-sync takes any word: prove the sync is computed, not stored

The verb now accepts a glyph-word and runs the Carrier16 state machine on it
step by step, so the four-lane sync tracks the input rather than replaying a
fixed trace. Demonstrated live: ⊢⊞⊥ syncs at step 3, ⊢≻⋈⊞⊥ moves the sync to
step 5, and ⊢⊤⊥⊤⊥ never syncs because it never engages. No argument still runs
the engaged word and the flat control side by side.
