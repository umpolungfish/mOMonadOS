erdos: the manuscripts become bootable

A publication needs its proof instantiated here so a reader can boot and follow
it, and only the Straus work was. Three more now are, under `erdos <name>`:

  schutte  searches all 32768 orientations of a six-vertex tournament, finds
           none dominating every pair, then builds the Paley tournament on seven
           and checks that it does
  landau   computes g(1..10) by the same complete partition descent the
           manuscript's generator uses, and shows g(7) coming from 4+3 rather
           than from the 7-cycle
  lcm      checks the quadratic lower bound n(n-1) <= lcm(1..n) and the
           logarithmic comparison it feeds

Every step computes its claim on this kernel. A step that printed a number it had
not derived would be a slideshow, which is the rule the bootstrap walk set and
this file keeps.
