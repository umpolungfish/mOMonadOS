straus ceiling: the rung records against n^(1/4)

The bound is the statement worth attacking, so the instrument now reads the
quantity a bound would have to supply. Over 5 <= n <= 400000 the records are
r = 3, 7, 11, 23, 31, 59 at n = 5, 49, 1129, 1201, 21169, 118801, and
r / floor(n^(1/4)) at those points is 3.0, 3.5, 2.2, 4.6, 2.6, 3.3 — no drift
across five orders of magnitude, maximum at 1201.

isqrt is Newton's, twice; the kernel has no libm and the integer root is the
honest quantity here rather than an approximation to a float one.
