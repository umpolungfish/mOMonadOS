straus — the ladder as an instrument, and the value it does not reach

nesting greedy reads Erdős–Straus as attracted with a finite budget; imasm check
on the two-fork form answers B and names the remedy — COMMIT one arm rather than
search it. This is what committing it gives, as a command.

A rung is r ≡ 3 (mod 4): the first term is 1/((n+r)/4), the remainder has
numerator exactly r, and the second term comes from a divisor d ≡ −1 (mod r) of
n·a. r = 3 is the greedy step; the higher rungs are the committed arms. Both
identities are proved in p4ramill; this module is the reading, and it checks each
representation it prints by cross-multiplying in u128 rather than trusting the
construction.

The spectrum over 5 ≤ n ≤ 4000, 666 values in the class: rung 3 closes 91.0%,
rung 7 another 6.8%, then 1.4%, 0.3%, 0.2%, 0.3% at rungs 11, 15, 19, 23. The
highest rung needed is 23, at n = 1201.

And one value is not reached by any rung up to 400: n = 2521. Naming it is the
point of the sweep — a count says a residue exists, the name says what the next
rung or the next mechanism has to answer for. It is not a counterexample to
anything: 4/2521 has representations, and what fails is this ladder's way of
finding one.

The instrument cannot settle the conjecture and says so where it prints: a
bounded spectrum across a range is evidence about the range, and the open
question is whether the height is bounded at all.
