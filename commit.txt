straus: the rung walk, read in the coordinate that is actually graded

I first read the walk as a distance — the least residue of M+u from zero — and
it collapsed to {0, 1/r}, which is a bool wearing a decimal. Two different n
produced identical tables, which is the tell.

The graded quantity is the SET the divisors reach: the residues M+u (mod r) as u
runs over the divisors of M². Its size against r is a density, the rung closes
exactly when zero is in it, and it separates. Over the 162 frontier values below
20000, coverage at the closing rung averages 0.734 and never falls below 0.533;
at a failing rung it averages 0.366 and clears 0.533 three times in 89. So the
walk is dissipative in this coordinate — the reached set grows into the target
rather than landing on it.

New verb straus defect <n>. Help now lists every verb and states the criterion
as u | M², which is what the instrument has been computing since the M² fix.
