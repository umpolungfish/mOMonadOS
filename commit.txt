erdos chordless and roth

chordless walks every chord of every odd cycle to length eleven. It failed first
at C5 with the chord (0,2), because I took the odd path and closed it, which
gives an even cycle. The odd cycle comes from the EVEN path plus the chord. The
walk caught the slip; the paper had it right.

roth finds the largest progression-free subset of [0,9) — size five, so any six
carry a progression — and builds Behrend's sphere in base seven over three
digits, where the largest single sphere carries six points.

Sixteen walks. Three manuscripts have none yet: the alternating prime series, the
Erdos-Fuchs average, and the multicolour Ramsey ratio.
