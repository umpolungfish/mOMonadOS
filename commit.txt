The kernel speaks the new alphabet

    ⊢ VINIT   ⊣ TANCH   > AFWD    < AREV    ⋈ CLINK   ⊙ IMSCRIB
    ∈ FSPLIT  ∋ FFUSE   ⊤ EVALT   ⊥ EVALF   ⊞ ENGAGR  ◻ IFIX

The wound torus reads ⊢⊙∈⊤⋈∋◻⊞<⋈⊣ now. `kernel_torus`, `triple_frame` and
`riemann_sic` carried their own copies of the glyph table and all three are on
the new set.

Two things the sweep turned up. `vita.rs` looked the fork and the fuse up in the
trunk's vocabulary by literal glyph, and `id_of` falls back to the LAST
vocabulary entry when it misses, so changing the alphabet would have silently
pointed the split and fuse legality masks at whatever sits at the end of the
vocabulary rather than failing. It takes the marks from the token now, so it
cannot drift again. The vocabulary itself is built in the same order from the
same tokens, so the trunk's weights still index what they were trained on; only
the rendered glyph changed.

`pericyclic_frobenoid` had the twelve names running against a shifted copy of the
alphabet: TANCH carried the fork's mark, CLINK the fuse's, FSPLIT and FFUSE the
two evaluators', and ENGAGR a tensor sign that is not an opcode at all. That was
wrong before this change and is right now.
