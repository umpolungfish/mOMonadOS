Circuit: put the RNA leg on the canonical correspondence, and report the drift

The glyph-to-codon map was a second construction: twelve off-diagonal base pairs
at the first two codon positions, enumerated. It closed, but it was not the
Grammar's map. GeneticCode.lean already states the correspondence as
aaToPrimitive and primitiveToAA, over the twelve promoted amino acids, with
promoted_card, ground_promoted_disjoint and twenty_eq_eight_plus_twelve behind
it. There is one Grammar; the enumeration was the copy and it is gone.

The RNA leg now reads codon → amino acid → primitive → axis → glyph, each step
off something that already existed. δ takes a glyph to the first codon in
enumeration order that carries it, giving a section that is a real RNA spelling
the alphabet: AUG UGG UGU UAU UUU AUU CAU AAU CAA GAU AAA GAA. Both circuits
close on it and μ∘δ = id holds on all twelve for RNA and for wasm.

Doing this surfaced a drift in AminoAcid::to_primitive, which derives a primitive
value from the codon box and is supposed to land on the same axis. On three amino
acids it does not: Tyr goes to a Criticality value, putting two amino acids on ⊙
and leaving < with none, and His and Asn are transposed. `circuit drift` reports
this live. The circuit uses the canonical correspondence; to_primitive is what
needs fixing, and is left alone here rather than changed underneath its other
callers.

The strand findings move, because the glyph assignment moved. Under the canonical
map:

- The CAG repeat is a run of pure ⊙. CAG is Gln and Gln is Criticality, so the
  polyglutamine expansion is unbounded repetition of the self-referential glyph.
  Its complement is silent, CUG being ground-layer Leu.
- Poly-A is not silent but constant: ⊞ in every frame, complement constant ⋈.
- The palindromic site GAAUUC remains a fixed point of the strand involution,
  now reading ◻⋈◻⋈ on both strands.
- The telomere repeat, Shine-Dalgarno and its recognizer are silent in every
  frame on both strands. Leu, Gly and Arg are ground layer.

The earlier readings of those sequences, and the claim that the antisense of the
section collapses to a control-flow triple, were artifacts of the constant wobble
the old section used. CIRCUIT.md carries the corrected findings.
