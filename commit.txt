Expose the gate model the threshold was hiding

eps_2q < 2.38e-8 was reported as a property of d=2048. It is a property of
n_gates(d), and n_gates(d) = d^2 was a modelling choice the single number
concealed. `d2048 crossover` names four models and varies them.

At d=2048 the required error spans 1.16e-11 to 4.88e-5 across the four, four
orders at one dimension, with d^2 landing on the 2.38e-8 the demonstration
reports. t_gate enters as a bare multiplier and is varied too rather than being
silently set to one.

Read against hardware the metric says something the single number did not.
Advantage at eps_2q = 1e-3 needs fewer than a hundred gates total, so the
largest dimension that clears it is 9 under d^2, 6 under d^2 log d, 4 under
d^3, and 99 under the cheapest floor of one gate per amplitude. d=2048 is out
of reach under every model, not only under the one that was chosen. Reporting
one threshold made that look like a fact about the hardware gap; it was a fact
about the model, and now both are on the surface.

The algebraic representation is what the extraction buys, and Step 5 measures
that: about thirteen bits per bit of d. This metric is not where it is bought,
and the report says so where it is printed.
