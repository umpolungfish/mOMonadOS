erdos: binomial and rep-tiling walks

binomial computes the gcd of the interior of each Pascal row to n = 30 and finds
p at every prime power and one elsewhere. reptiling searches for the first n that
is neither a square, a sum of two squares, nor three times a square, and finds
six, then prints the classification below twenty: blocked at 6, 7, 11, 14, 15, 19.

The rep-tiling walk had a second step checking that n copies of area 1/n fill
area 1. That is arithmetic, not a result, and a walk that prints one is the
slideshow this file exists to avoid. It now prints which n pass.
