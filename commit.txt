Vox: decode the bytes, so the auditor can read a binary

Vox took an instruction list nothing produced. parse_elf handed back raw bytes
and there was no path from bytes to instructions, so recompile_function,
compute_merges, emit_word and roundtrip_check all had a parameter that could not
be filled. Vox could audit a word typed at the prompt, not a program.

vox_decode.rs is the missing leg: an x86-64 length decoder with control-flow
recognition. It recovers the length, so the walk stays in phase, and enough of
the shape to name a mnemonic the classifier already understands. Registers and
operand detail are not reconstructed, because the glyph does not depend on them.

An unknown opcode returns None. The walk stops and reports how far it got, so a
partial lift always reads as partial. This is the load-bearing choice: a length
decoder that guesses a width goes out of phase and keeps emitting instructions,
the word still verdicts, and the verdict is fiction. Refusing makes the failure
visible. Each refusal names the bytes, so extending the table is mechanical:
coverage on /bin/ls went 15% to 41% to 44% to 100% over four rounds, the stops
being string operations, the 0F BA bit-test group, the whole x87 D8..DF escape,
and xchg with the accumulator.

Two defects surfaced underneath.

parse_elf read sh_type at section-header offset +0, which is sh_name. Nothing
ever matched SHT_PROGBITS, so it always found zero executable sections and the
ELF path had never run. Both the 64-bit and 32-bit field offsets were shifted by
one field.

compute_merges looked up predecessors and addresses by linear scan, making the
lift quadratic. It finished on small inputs and hung on a real .text.
Predecessor counts now use a BTreeMap and address membership a binary search
over the ascending address list. The 517k-instruction section that hung lifts in
under two seconds.

Coverage is now 100% of bytes with no stops on /bin/true, /bin/ls, /bin/bash,
/usr/bin/git, /usr/bin/python3, and on the kernel's own binary: 690,031
instructions across 3.2MB of its own .text.

Verdicts distribute by shape. The _init thunk closes at T, opening a fork and
fusing it before the anchor. PLT stubs run N, linear with no fork. A whole .text
read as one word sits at B, a fork still open at a terminal.

Also cleared the two warnings vox.rs carried: an unused ToString import and an
unused binding in emit_word. Findings in VOX.md.
