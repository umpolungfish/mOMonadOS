A word that is not a braid on n strands is refused, not truncated.

B_n has generators sigma_1 .. sigma_{n-1}, so on three strands there is no
sigma_3. Given one, the evaluator stopped at it and returned the product of the
prefix, while the writhe was still summed over the whole word. A 33-crossing
word containing sigma_3 on three strands reported the invariant of its first
five crossings, and reported it in the same format and with the same confidence
as a correct answer. Nothing about the output said a word had been discarded.

validate_braid_word rejects with the offending generator, the range that does
exist, and the strand count the word actually needs. The matrix loop skips
rather than breaks, so a missed validation degrades visibly instead of
plausibly.
