The evaluation point is stated as a winding, because it is one.

The report read "V at t=e^(2 pi i/5)" — the same number written in the units the
model does not use, in a kernel that had just been converted to carry phases as
rational turns. It reads "V at t = 1/5 winding" now, taken from the lattice
constant rather than spelled out.

The value's own phase is reported in windings as well, which makes the
chirality verdict self-evidencing. The knots that cannot be told from their
mirror come out at exactly 1/2 and 0/1, the only self-inverse windings, and a
separated one lands off-lattice. The criterion is now visible in the output
instead of resting on a tolerance applied to an imaginary part somewhere out of
sight.

winding_of snaps to the lattice when the value is on it, trying the
denominators the model actually uses, and falls back to thousandths rather than
claiming an exactness it does not have.
