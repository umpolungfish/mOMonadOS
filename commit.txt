Nothing hardcoded in the IUFT gates

Twelve angle triples sat in iuft_qc.rs beside the encoder that computes them, and they had
drifted. The graviton's differed from its own catalog tuple by 0.397 and the electron's by
0.629 — and the electron's comment said why: "using encode: θ=180°, φ=105°, ψ=90° — but we
refine from IUFT expansion". A hand refinement of a computed value is a second source of
truth, and the second source is the one that goes stale.

They did more damage than drift. `gate_for` consulted the table first, so `iuft encode
graviton` printed the hand-written triple under the word "encoded" and never reached the
tuple. And they made the consistency check vacuous: for a name with no triple, `gate_for`
fell through to the catalog, the check encoded the same entry a second time, and the two
agreed to the last bit. `iuft verify yhwh` printed 0.000000 and meant nothing by it — the
one line that looked like a pass was the only one carrying no information.

The triples are gone and no reference list replaced them. Names go through
`catalog::lookup`, which already holds the kernel's normalisation and aliases, so "CLINK
L8", "clink_l8" and "CLINK-L8" reach one entry without a second alias table. `nearest_known`
ranges over the catalog rather than twelve hand-picked names, which is what "nearest known"
was supposed to mean. `iuft list [domain]` encodes whatever the catalog holds. `iuft matrix`
takes the names to compare.

`iuft verify` asks the two questions that survive. Every entry must encode to a unitary
gate: 249 of 249 do. And the encoding is a 12→3 projection, degenerate by construction, so
where it sends different tuples to the same gate is the projection's kernel showing itself:
94 colliding pairs among 249 entries. Among them, neutron and proton, which both encode to
θ=110.0° φ=260.0° ψ=90.0°. The hardcoded table had given them 75/285/90 and 72.3/30/90 —
two hand-written values papering over a degeneracy the projection actually has.

Also re-tokenizes the iuft tail. The line arrives split with a field limit, so `iuft matrix
a b c` saw one name and `iuft verify CLINK L8` could not see an entry whose name has a space.
