The catalog subset regenerates on the new primitive keys

The twelve primitive axes now carry the IMASM alphabet, and the catalog this
kernel bakes in is keyed by them. `tools/gen_catalog_subset.py` holds the slot
list and the enum-prefix map, so both move there and `src/catalog_ask_subset.rs`
is regenerated rather than edited: 8,016 entries, one more than the committed
snapshot and none fewer.

⊙ stays on Criticality, so `Phi_` keeps its slot in the prefix map and the 5,122
entries whose criticality value is the monad are untouched.

`src/vita.rs` listed eleven of the twelve axes as literal glyphs for the trunk's
vocabulary, with ⊙ absent because it is injected separately as a value token.
Those eleven are renamed in place; the ordering is unchanged, so vita_weights.bin
still indexes what it was trained on.

Boot still reports ordinal faithfulness against the Lean canonical, which is the
cross-language check that the ordinals did not move with the keys.
