Wire the secp256k1_unwinder verb and add the vox hex lift lane

The secp256k1_unwinder dispatch arm (word | steps | mapping | walk | verdict |
tuple | constants) over the ported module, and a new vox hex sub-lane beside
evm and wasm: vox hex <hex> decodes a raw machine-code hex value stream with
vox_core::lanes::from_hex, loads the bytes as a flat code image, walks the
control flow, recompiles each function to its glyph word, and reads the closure
verdict. Hosted real plus a bare-metal stub, mirroring vox_lift_file.

Verified against the main Vox dir and G-mOMonadOS: a conditional branch
cmp/jne/xor/ret reads T with the fork and fuse reconnecting, and the shuffle
control decodes to different words, identical readings across all three builds.
