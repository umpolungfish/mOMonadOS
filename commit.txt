The winding lattice becomes load-bearing, and the suite checks it.

Holding the lattice in constants that only the Jones prefactors and a print
statement consulted left it decorative: it could be dropped without anything
failing, and it was, once, in the course of an evening. theta(j) already
computed h = j(j+1)/(k+2), a rational number of turns, and then pushed it
through radians; it now returns that rational directly as a Winding, and the
R-symbol for the tau channel is WIND_R_TAU rather than a hand-written
7*pi/5. Removing the lattice now breaks the algebra.

verify_all gains a tenth check as its tenth entry: every phase native to the
model must land on the tenths of a winding, the declared theta must agree with
the computed one, and 0 and 1/2 must be the only self-inverse windings. This is
an invariant of the theory rather than a convenience, since the braid
generator's eigenvalues are 4/10 and -3/10 and they generate the lattice. If a
phase ever leaves the tenths, either the level changed or something is being
computed in the wrong units, and both should be loud.

Regression after rewiring: all ten checks pass on the metal, the generator
defect holds at 3.3e-16, the five Jones values are unchanged with the unknot at
exactly zero error, and T still compiles to 5.4143e-6.
