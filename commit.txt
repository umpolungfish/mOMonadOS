No caps: every limit replaced by the condition that ends the loop

I had numbers standing in for reasoning, and each one was hiding something.

nesting ran its confirming nest to a 10,000-step ceiling. It now stops when the
gap stops shrinking, which is the run reporting the answer rather than me
waiting for one, and a map that never arrives says so at the step that shows it.
The edge is stated in the code: a map converging non-monotonically would be
stopped at its widening, and none offered here does that.

ctc had two `> 8` breaks in loops that already terminate on their own — the
value walk by cycle detection, the set lift by saturation. Dead rails, removed.

substrate scanned to weight 64 for a critical value and reported "none up to the
search bound", which is not the same claim as "none". The ranking is
sub·w + family, so past the largest family gap between tokens whose substrate
scores differ, no ordering can flip again. sequence.rs now derives that bound
and substrate scans to it, so none means none. It comes out at 36 for the seed
and 35 for the variants, which my 64 happened to clear — the old result stands,
now by construction instead of by luck.

run_serial_cmds.sh killed qemu at 60s. A vita turn takes longer than that on
emulated bare metal, so the harness was reporting a hang that was a computation.
Removed.

And the one that was hiding under the others. f64_sqrt was Newton with exactly
ten iterations from z = x. For a small argument the first step overshoots to
near 0.5 and ten halvings cannot come back, so sqrt(4.9e-19) answered about
5e-4 — five orders out. Every small distance compared against a tolerance was
wrong. It now starts by halving the exponent in the bit pattern and runs until
the sequence stops descending, which terminates on its own at full precision.
`nesting halve 203` settled in 60 steps before and settles in 37 now.

Help, which I should have written first. All four take `help` and answer for
themselves, and all four have submenus naming every form they accept rather
than the bare `submenu: None` I left. ctc's help computes each action's fixed
points as it prints them, so the table cannot drift from the code.
