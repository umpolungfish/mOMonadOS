The sic verbs are discoverable and the serial runner stops swallowing their output.

run_serial_cmds.sh piped everything through a sed program written for the d2048 sections, so any other command's output vanished; the filter is now opt-in through FILTER and output passes through by default. The sic fallback help listed two verbs where there are ten, so the moduli commands could not be found from inside the REPL. quadratic.rs gains ray_order_at, which is what lets the d=16 tower print computed orders instead of a written-out table.
