Re-census, because the audit was reading yesterday

`redteam audit erdos` and `prov erdos` read a table baked into the kernel, and it
was stamped 2026-08-13 — before today's work on Problems 3 and 41. An audit
reading a stale census reports on a corpus that no longer exists, and it does it
without complaining, which is the worst way for a check to be wrong.

Regenerated: 11323 files, and the Erdős selector now sees 95 files and 1051
theorems where it saw 61 and 933.

What it says now, which is worth writing down rather than acting on blind:

276 sorry against 1051 theorems. 19 axiom declarations, one of them the
Bloom–Sisask axiom added today, which is the point of naming an assumption —
it shows up in the count instead of hiding inside a false density claim.

83 theorems close by native_decide, which runs compiled code outside the Lean
kernel's checked path, and 155 of 1051 close by finite evaluation at all. A
finite closure holds for its decidable instance and says nothing past it.

Six basenames appear more than once. The proof-vehicle snapshots explain some of
it legitimately, but not all: inside ig-docs, erdos_problem_44_corrected/ holds
byte-identical copies of ErdosFormalize, ErdosFormalize2 and ErdosFormalize3 from
erdos_formalize*/, and erdos_problems/ErdosProblems.lean is byte-identical to the
one beside it. The audit's highest-risk dependency — 49 theorems, 48 of them
sorry — is one of those duplicates, so it is counted twice.

MoDoT/lean/ErdosProblems.lean and ig-docs/erdos_problem_44_sidon_extension/ are
the same 1829 lines and differ only in notation: Γ, φ̂, Ω where the other reads
∈, ⊙, ◻. The ig-docs copy predates the mark migration. That is not local — 730
files outside the proof vehicles still carry the old spelling, and I am not
converting them by pattern, because Γ and Ω are also just Γ and Ω.
