straus cof: the height at which a cofactor of M appears

The square stratum is the set of n whose divisor lives in M^2 and not in M, so
the quantity that defines it is the rung height searched without a cofactor
arriving. straus cof <n> <maxrung> reads it. 2521 has none to rung 4000, which
settles it as a stratum member rather than a value beyond a small budget; 196561
gets one at rung 75 and 1201 at rung 23.
