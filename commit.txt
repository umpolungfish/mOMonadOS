Built gpu_crystal_full_space.rs. Phase_5 of gpu_native_build_of_momonados
names memory_mechanism as "a 17.28-million-entry Crystal type space
navigated by address lookup," and nothing built so far actually touched
that space -- gpu_catalog_crystal.rs and gpu_imasm_cycle.rs both batch the
live catalog, 8667 entries, the populated fraction of it, not the claim.
This checks the claim at its own scale: every one of the 17,280,000
addresses crystal::TOTAL names, decode(addr) into its 12-axis tuple then
re-encode, on the GPU. Cross-checked against the CPU's own crystal::decode/
encode on 2.16 million of them, not trusted from internal self-agreement,
since a GPU kernel that only agrees with itself proves nothing if it shares
a bug with its own inverse.

Ran live on the RTX 4070: 0 round-trip failures across all 17,280,000
addresses, 0 mismatches against the CPU sample. The full address space this
ob3ect claims is coherent at the scale it actually names, not the 8667-entry
subset everything before this checked.
