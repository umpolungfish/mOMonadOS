A Quantum category, and examples that are actually printed

Three separate problems, one visible symptom.

The quantum commands had no home. fibqc, quantum_compile, jones_polynomial, bg, shor,
iuft, hqe, dyson, troq, afdmc, hop, manifold, triple, sic, d12 and d2048 were scattered
through the Grammar menu or nowhere, so nothing named the kernel's quantum surface as a
surface. They are a Quantum category now, and they are removed from Grammar rather than
listed twice.

MenuItem has carried an `example` field all along and nothing ever read it. Every command
had one; none was displayed. Both the category view and the single-command view print it
now.

The top-level help hardcoded about twenty commands out of ninety-two. It walks MAIN_MENU
instead, so a command added to a menu appears without anyone editing the help text, and
an unknown topic checks the menu for a category and then for a command before falling
through to search.

Two repairs came out of the build. src/ovm.rs had the whole impl BlochVec body duplicated
— an orphaned copy of norm, normalize, dot, rot_z, rot_so3 and scale with no impl header,
closing with a brace that matched nothing. It was a mis-offset insertion, twice, and it
broke every downstream module.

And build_bootimage.sh piped cargo into grep and then appended `|| true`, so the exit
status was grep's and then discarded; it checked only that the ELF existed, which a stale
one does. It printed Done over a failed compile. It now takes the status from PIPESTATUS
and says the ELF is stale when the build fails.
