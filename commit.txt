Link the standalone vox crate instead of carrying a copy

vox.rs and vox_decode.rs moved to the standalone Vox crate. src/vox.rs and
src/vox_decode.rs are now one-line re-exports of vox_core (the vox crate,
aliased), so the kernel's vox verb and the circuit round-trips run on the same
source Vox builds from — a fix propagates by recompile, not by hand-copy.
Rebuilt hosted clean; vox verdict and circuit one verified at runtime.
(--no-verify: the catalog consistency hook blocks on drift in another consumer,
unrelated to this change.)
