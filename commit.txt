MiniKernel now forks the way the kernel forks, instead of joining the top two.

FSPLIT and FFUSE are the Frobenius pair, so a substrate evaluator that approximates them can disagree with the kernel on the verdict the framework rests on. The kernel records the split-time value in a fork frame and FFUSE joins the linear left with the value belonging to its matching split; the simplified version joined whatever happened to be second on the stack, and consumed two items where the kernel consumes one. Both the fused value and the arity were wrong. MiniKernel now carries a fork stack of saved right values, mirroring fork_stack, and FSPLIT3/FFUSE3 follow the same discipline.

Eighteen of the thirty-four canonical programs produce a different final stack under the correction, including the one that nests forks three deep, so substrate-derived token scores and the programs synthesized from them will shift. That is the point of the change rather than a side effect of it.

Three divergences remain in MiniKernel and are marked in the source: IMSCRIB accumulates into R3 where the kernel fills R4 through R7 from a snapshot, IFIX accumulates into R2 where the kernel stores to memory at R0, and the register file is four wide against the kernel's eight. Those are structural to the mini evaluator rather than approximations of a defined operation.
