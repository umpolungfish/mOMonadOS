Phases are held in windings, where one winding is a full turn.

Every phase native to this model is an exact multiple of a tenth of a turn:
theta_tau and R^{tau tau}_1 at 4/10, R^{tau tau}_tau at -3/10, the Jones root
at 2/10, the framing phase at -1/10, the loop value's phase at 5/10, the
modular T diagonal at 0 and 4/10, the F eigenvalues at 0 and 5/10. Carrying
these in radians converts exact rationals into transcendentals, multiplies
them, and then measures the drift.

`Winding` holds a phase as a rational number of turns and composes by integer
arithmetic. jones_polynomial now builds both prefactors from it: alpha^writhe
is one exact rational scaling rather than `writhe` complex multiplications, and
the loop value splits into an exact half-turn phase times a real magnitude, so
the phase accumulates nothing. The unknot comes back at exactly zero error, the
real-valued knots at 1e-17 to 1e-16, and the T(2,n) period-ten closure holds to
1e-15.

The braid generator's eigenvalues, 4/10 and -3/10, generate the tenths. That is
the same fact as det(sigma_1) being a primitive tenth root of unity, which was
noted earlier only as a weak checksum; it is the lattice.

What this states plainly is why compilation is hard at all. T is 1/8 of a turn
and S is 1/4, and 1/8 is not a multiple of 1/10, so no braid reaches the T gate
exactly at any length. Solovay-Kitaev exists to approach an incommensurable
point on a commensurate lattice, and what makes the approach possible is the
non-commutativity rather than the phases.

Winding::is_self_inverse is the chirality criterion from the lattice rather
than a tolerance on an imaginary part: 0 and 1/2 are the only self-inverse
windings, and those are exactly the real values a mirror cannot be told from.

`fibqc winding` prints the lattice.
