nesting greedy — Erdős–Straus as a reading, not a description

The instrument only knew five maps, so no conjecture could be posed to it. The
greedy Fibonacci–Sylvester step is now one of them: from x, subtract the largest
unit fraction that fits. Nesting 4/n in it puts Erdős–Straus in the ATTRACTED
bin with a finite budget — the orbit always arrives, and the conjecture is the
claim that the budget is three. That distinction is what the reading buys, and
it is what sent the Lean work at the denominator structure instead of at a
search.
