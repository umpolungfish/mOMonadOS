The quantity to watch in Erdős 41, and it is not flat

Added a fourth step to the triple-sums walk: not whether the counting bound
holds, but how much room is left in it.

The bound C(k,3) ≤ 3N says a size-k set needs N ≳ k³/18. The walk finds the
least N that actually admits one and reads k³/N off it: 15, 15, 14, 12 for
k = 5, 6, 7, 8. Counting allows 18, so the sets are harder to build than counting
requires, and increasingly so.

That is the quantity the conjecture is about. If k grows like a constant times
N^(1/3), k³/N settles on a constant; if k is o(N^(1/3)), it goes to zero. Four
points do not decide anything and I am not claiming they do — the sequence is
built by exhaustive descent and stops at N = 40 because the descent does. What
they establish is that the ratio is the right thing to compute and that it is
descending rather than sitting still.

Also asked blackbox for a law, twice: on k(N) itself and on the thresholds
1,2,3,4,8,14,23,40. It licensed no prediction either time, which is the correct
answer — neither sequence is polynomial, and a fitted law would have been an
artifact of the hypothesis class rather than a fact about B₃ sets.
