substrate: closure constant, content bifurcating

I recorded the weight scan as having chosen the wrong observable, because cycle
length came back as one at every weight and a constant column locates no
critical point. It is the right observable. An entire one-parameter family in
the one-shot class with no member in a basin is the signature of a conservative
action, and that is a stronger statement than inertness: closure cannot move
because every parameter is already at the fixed point.

The content does bifurcate, at w_c = 1, and the two facts are compatible
because they are not the same question. Closure asks whether the program
returns to itself. Content asks which program it returns to. The transition is
a change of sector, not of closure — the program crosses from one self-closing
regime to another and never fails to close on the way.

The variant sweep is what was not visible before. A critical weight exists only
where the family matrix leads with self-imscription. Step off the
self-referential topology or narrow the range and the leader is already the
advance, the substrate vote has nothing to overturn, and no weight to 64 moves
anything. What the vote does is carry a self-referential tuple out of pure
self-imscription, and on this evidence nothing else.

The weight, the builder and the self-imscriber are the kernel's own. Only the
reading is new.
