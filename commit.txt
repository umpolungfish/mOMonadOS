Mirror G-mOMonadOS: full secp256k1 arithmetic with recover and verify

Replaced the descriptive-only secp256k1_unwinder module with the full one from
G-mOMonadOS, byte-identical: the 19-step walk types kept, plus the public-key
to-secret-key arithmetic (U256 field ops, curve points, decompress,
recover_private_key, verify_keypair). Wired the two key-I/O subcommands into the
verb to match:

  secp256k1_unwinder recover <pubkey_hex> [start] [max_k]   key in, private key out
  secp256k1_unwinder verify <sk_hex> <pk_hex>               check a keypair

No search cap: max_k defaults to the full u64 range. Builds bare-metal and
hosted. Verified on the generator vector: verify 1 <G> is true, recover <G>
returns private key 1, identical to G-mOMonadOS. The two repos now mirror.
