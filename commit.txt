erdos mantel: the balanced count, and what one edge past it forces

Two steps. The first checks that the balanced bipartite graph's edge count is
exactly the floor of n^2/4, as an identity to n = 40 rather than as a quotation.
The second searches every graph on 4, 5 and 6 vertices carrying one edge more
than that bound and finds none triangle-free.

Seven walks now: schutte, landau, lcm, sumset, ramsey33, binomial, reptiling,
mantel.
