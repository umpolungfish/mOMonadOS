erdos lenz and syndetic

lenz builds rational points on each of the two orthogonal circles from
Pythagorean triples and checks in integer arithmetic that all sixteen cross pairs
sit at squared distance exactly one, then counts the pairs against the floor of
n^2/4. The first step originally computed 1 + 1 = 2 in a loop, which is the same
tautology the rep-tiling walk had and no more a proof of the geometry than
printing it.

syndetic runs the duality over every subset of a window of sixteen: the longest
run in a set is the longest gap in its complement, 65536 checks, agreeing every
time. Then the powers of two, whose gaps double.

Twelve walks: schutte, landau, lcm, sumset, ramsey33, binomial, reptiling,
mantel, kac, sdr, lenz, syndetic.
