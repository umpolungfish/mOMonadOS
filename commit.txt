straus kshift: the rung read off k*n+1, and straus reach

Two verbs. straus reach reports how deep the cofactor list goes — the least k
with k*r-1 dividing M — and shows the depth is a long tail, not a short list.
straus kshift inverts it: for each k it factors k*n+1 instead of M, which needs
no knowledge of a, and reports which k supplies the rung.

Also fixed the argument parsing for four-field straus commands: the REPL splits a
line into four fields, so the trailing two numbers arrive as one string.
