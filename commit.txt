Short names for the two most-typed quantum commands

quantum_compile and jones_polynomial were chosen so an agent that knew the grammar-tool
names would find them in the kernel. That reason still holds, but they are long to type
for the two commands most likely to be typed repeatedly.

qc and jp are the primary names now. Both long forms remain as match arms, so nothing
that used them breaks and the grammar-name mapping is intact. Top-level qc was free; the
existing qc is a subcommand of rebis materials.

Verified: qc H T 4, jp 1 1 1, quantum_compile H 4 and jones_polynomial 1 1 all run.
