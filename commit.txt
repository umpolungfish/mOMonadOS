vita always builds; ctc and nesting take arguments

vita was in the menu and out of the binary at the same time. The command sat
behind a `vita` feature, the module behind the same gate, and no build enabled
it, so typing vita answered "Unknown: vita" while help listed it. The weights
were on disk the whole time. Gate removed from the module, the arm, the Cargo
feature and the menu text; vita_weights.bin is baked in unconditionally, which
is what takes the kernel from 2.9M to 5.3M. It answers, and its turn is
admitted. It needs more than the 60s run_serial_cmds.sh allows, which is a
property of a neural turn on emulated bare metal, not a fault.

ctc and nesting were glossaries. They took no arguments and printed a fixed
table of six and seven cases I had chosen, so neither could be pointed at
anything. They described general instruments and implemented a printout. Both
now take a pairing and answer about it: `ctc <action> <T|F|N|B>` over the six
Belnap actions, `nesting <map> <x> [y]` over the five maps, each bare form
falling back to the full sweep. Unknown names are refused with what is
available rather than silently doing something else. The menu examples show
real arguments instead of repeating the command name.

Nothing about the mathematics changed. What changed is that two of the four are
no longer demonstrations of themselves.
