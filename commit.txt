Accept uncompressed (04) public keys in decompress

decompress handled compressed 02/03 and a bare 64-hex x, but a 04-prefixed
uncompressed key (04 || x || y) fell through to None, so recover reported no
key when the real issue was an unparsed input. Added the uncompressed branch:
both coordinates are given, so it reads x and y straight off with no square
root. Verified: the uncompressed k=2 key recovers 2, and the compressed 02/03
keys still recover correctly.
