The d=2048 degree is computed in the kernel, and the heap survives the sweeps.

The propagation report had both 2^20 and 2^21 in it and neither was computed. Both are now derived from d: the class number by cycles of reduced forms, and the degree as the unit group of O/p2^k modulo the global units, where the conductor exponent comes from v2(d)+1 and the unit group order from q^(k-1)(q-1) for an inert two. The fundamental unit at that discriminant is far too large to hold, so it is never formed; the continued fraction of omega has P and Q bounded throughout, which lets the period be found exactly while the convergents are carried modulo the conductor, giving epsilon as a residue. The result is 2^20 over F, matching the value reached by the other route.

Two failures found on the way. The report reached the class number through ray_data, which enumerates the unit group of O/m as m^2 pairs: at m = 6144 that is six hundred megabytes, and the kernel panicked. Enumeration now has a budget and RayData carries a flag saying whether it was computed, with the scope report marking what falls outside. Second, the allocator is a bump with LIFO reclaim only, so the working vectors of a sweep accumulated until the heap was gone; since RayData is all scalars, each computation now runs inside heap_mark and heap_reset.

d=44 is documented rather than fixed. Requiring the generator coprime to q and orienting it into p squared both leave the count at sixteen against forty, so what remains is the extension class, which the order-two model does not determine there.
