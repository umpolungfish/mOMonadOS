Rationalize the monomial: the cancellation was in the reading

Last change found that the expanded triple could not be read in f64 and then
labelled it, which fixes nothing. The cancellation was never in the number. It
was in the choice to evaluate a + b*sqrt(D) when those two terms agree through
twenty digits. Multiply above and below by the conjugate and it is gone:

    M = (a + b*sqrt(D))/c   =>   M = c*Norm(M) / (a - b*sqrt(D))

The denominator now ADDS two terms of the same sign, so nothing cancels and
f64 carries it at full relative precision, and the numerator is an exact
integer. Reading the exact triple gives 0.000488281016884806, correct in every
digit printed.

Norm is carried multiplicatively through the product -- 1 * (-2045)^3 * 2049^2
-- rather than recovered afterwards from a^2 - b^2*D, which needs 5.4e39 where
i128 stops at 1.7e38.

The factored evaluation stays as a second, independent route to the same
number, and the roles have swapped: rationalizing the exact triple is now the
ACCURATE reading at fifteen digits, and multiplying the factors is the one that
drifts, at thirteen. Agreement between the two is the check that the i128
expansion is the monomial it claims to be.

The naive reader is deleted rather than kept with a warning on it. A function
that returns noise on the input it exists to serve is not a function with a
caveat.

Monomial 0.000488281016884806 against 1/d = 0.00048828125, gap -2.331e-10.
fibqc verify all pass. Both trees carry the same file.
