straus nest / census — the ladder classified by the nesting rule

A new creature beside `nesting` and `ctc`: it reads one n against the ladder and
names which of the rule's three classes the pairing falls in.

  one-shot   the divisor is fixed by the congruence for every n in its residue
             class, so nothing is searched. u = 2 at rung 3 is one: 3 ∣ n²+8
             holds identically for 3∤n, and 2 ∣ M exactly when n ≡ 5 (mod 8).
  iterated   a divisor exists but the rung has to be walked to find it.
  no closure nothing lands inside the budget — which on a conservative action
             means the stabiliser set being read is wrong, not that the value is
             far away. That reading is what found the M² correction earlier.

`straus census 5 4000`: 50.2% one-shot, 49.8% iterated, 0% no closure. The
one-shot half is proved in p4ramill as straus_one_shot.
