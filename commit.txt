straus kceiling: the least k, read from the rung side

Searching k and factoring k*n+1 truncates: k reaches (M+1)/r. Reading rungs
instead and taking k = (w+1)/r from each cofactor gives the whole spectrum. Over
the frontier below 200000: least k at most 2 for 314 values, at most 8 for 565,
at most 32 for 613, tail to 12813 at n = 66529. One value, 2521, has no cofactor
at any rung up to 200.
