The gate constants are named for the axes they gate

Every gate held a specification in the marks and was reached through an identifier
in the retired vocabulary: phi for parity, odot for criticality, omega for
protection, and single letters for dimensionality, topology, recognition, fidelity,
kinetics, granularity, grammar, chirality and stoichiometry. Thirty-three constants
renamed to the axis each one gates, so the source reads in the same vocabulary as
the output.

Two criticality gates were named for what they are rather than for an ordinal that
collided with a local binding: the exceptional-point threshold and the subcritical
one that always passes.
