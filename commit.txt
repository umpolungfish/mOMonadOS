Boot the Erdős 3 ring instead of trusting its decimal

The settled ring came out of the host tools: click, certify, the L9 lateral, a
catalog sweep, forge, anneal. That is a transcript, and a transcript has to be
trusted — `witness erdos` said it plainly, "smallest executable object: none in
the kernel". So the ring is computed here now, and it computes rather than
prints.

The ring is erdos_conjecture_ap · gowers_u3_norm · behrend_construction ·
gowers_u3_norm, closing head to tail on R↔S at O∞. The U³ unit appears twice
because two units cannot cyclize and three cyclize but store strain; doubling it
flattens the spectrum and the anneal takes the strain to zero.

Its adjacency weighs a clean bond 1 and a cross-link by its reaction centres, so
bonds 2–3 and 3–4 weigh 2. That matrix satisfies A⁴ = 10·A² in integers, which is
λ⁴ = 10λ² in matrix form, so the whole spectrum is settled without a float: λ is
0 or ±√10. tr(A²) = 20 = 2ρ² gives ρ² = 10 exactly. And the gap is zero because
the nonzero spectrum is ±ρ — |λ₂| = ρ by the pairing, not by rounding.

That last one matters for how the result reads. A zero gap is the settled state,
not a missing one: no mode is privileged, the ring leans on no single strut. It
is the reason the verdict is SETTLED rather than SHAKY, and the three-unit ring
was SHAKY — gap 0.732, strain 0.250, which is the state proofs come out shaky in.

A spectral claim carried as 3.1623 would have to be believed. Carried as
A⁴ = 10·A² it can be checked, and now it is, on boot.
