erdos kac: the window that carries no prime power

Two steps. The first checks that omega = 1 holds exactly at the prime powers to
n = 2000 — the reason an interval on which omega exceeds one excludes them. The
second finds the longest such window below two thousand, [1332, 1360] of length
twenty-nine, and confirms it carries none.

Nine walks: schutte, landau, lcm, sumset, ramsey33, binomial, reptiling, mantel,
kac.
