Close the braid dual, and give it a door

braid_to_imasm and read_tangle were written as δ and μ of the same pair and then
neither exposed nor checked. The reason they were never checked is structural:
the kernel is a bare-metal binary with no library target, so there was nowhere a
round trip could run. A dual that has never been closed is a claim.

So the lib target comes first. It carries only tokens and braid_protocol,
nothing that touches serial, interrupts, the entry point or the halt, and it
takes main.rs's own no_std condition because cargo builds it alongside the
binary. The bare-metal image still builds and is unchanged.

The gate is seven tests and they pass. mu(delta) = id holds on the GENERATOR
WORD, which is the object, and not on the token stream: delta chooses a depth
path between crossings and mu does not record which path was taken, so a program
is one presentation of a braid rather than the braid. Claiming the stronger
identity would be claiming something false. Writhe and crossing count survive.
The two handednesses cost the same and differ by exactly one gate, and that gate
is AREV, the involution that swaps T and F, swaps t and f, and fixes B and N.
Chirality in the braid is chirality in the register.

Yang-Baxter and far commutativity are checked as agreements on the invariants mu
recovers, which is what they can be here: the two sides are distinct programs, so
the relation was never going to be an identity of token streams.

fibqc braid and fibqc tangle expose the two directions, with the mu-of-delta
result printed on the same line so a caller sees the closure rather than
trusting it. Both land in the reference in this change, as section 14b, together
with the reading of Markov's first move as ROTAT rather than as something
analogous to it, and with the expected refusal: braid generators are invertible,
IFIX and the evaluator projections are not, so the braid group embeds only into
the involutive part and the surplus is measurement.
