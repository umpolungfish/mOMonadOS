Charted gpu_sixteen3_tensor_kernel's real scaling, 10^4 to 10^9 register
triples. Added internal Instant timing to the module itself (generate,
htod+launch+sync, dtoh, CPU verify) rather than time the whole process
including boot, so a sweep across seven orders of magnitude is comparable
point to point. Ran it once per n, one boot per point, real numbers in
measurements/gpu_sixteen3_tensor_kernel_scaling.csv.

The chart makes the shape plain: the GPU stage itself is under a percent
of total time once n gets large, the CPU loop that verifies every result
against imasm_core dominates by roughly 30:1 at the billion scale, which
matches the 34.6s wall-clock Lando measured directly. The 10^4/10^5 points
are single-sample and noisy, said so in the README rather than smoothed
over -- the ~30:1 ratio from 10^6 up is the real, repeatable finding.
