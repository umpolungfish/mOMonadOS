Give the CFG figure the kernel's geometry, and a clear field

Three things were wrong with the picture the dialect visualizer produced.

The layout was a circle. Every token sat at an arbitrary angle, so the drawing
said nothing the token order had not already said. The real arrangement is fixed
and is read off k3vM.html: the FSPLIT shell is centred on IMSCRIB with radius LR;
the evaluator sphere carries the SAME radius and is centred one radius along +x,
so IMSCRIB lies exactly on it and the two are tangent; the three evaluators stand
at 120 degrees on the evaluator equator, which is the A2 trine; FFUSE sits at the
antipodal pole. Both shells and the coupler between their centres are drawn now,
and a token's position is a fact about what it is. Tokens the geometry does not
pin ride the syzygy axis in program order, and a token occurring twice is fanned
rather than stacked invisibly on itself.

The opcodes were the retired spellings. They are the legal twelve now, in both
the Python table and the emitted JavaScript one.

And the figure could not be read on a page. The titles are gone, inside the SVG
and above it, because the universe name and the gate verdict are the caption's
job and drawn there they sat on top of the geometry. The body and the figure
surface are transparent. The four family colours were picked to glow on
near-black and three of them were too light to survive a white field, so they
are the Okabe-Ito equivalents at the same hue. The node discs lost their dark
fill, which had swallowed the glyph.

The reason none of the colour work appeared to take: a CSS rule set fill and
font-size on every text element, and a CSS rule beats an SVG presentation
attribute, so each glyph was painted near-white at 8px no matter what the drawing
code asked for. On a clear field that is invisible. The rule no longer sets
either, and the per-element attributes decide.

shoot_cfg.py captures the figure element on a transparent field. A page is not a
figure, and the alternative was a second stripped copy of the generator that
would have drifted from this one within a week.
