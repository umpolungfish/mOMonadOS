repl_compile scoped itself with heap_mark/heap_reset; repl_jones never did. Both
sectors build F(n) x F(n) complex matrices for every generator, about 600 KB at
ten strands, and the bump allocator reclaims only in LIFO order — so each
invocation kept its representation forever. Ten calls in a row exhausted the
8 MB and the allocator then failed a zero-byte request, which is what
"memory allocation of 0 bytes failed" was.

Scoped now, so the sequence that panicked runs indefinitely and interleaves with
other calls without growth.

Also capped at 16 strands. The fusion space is F(n-1) and the matrices are its
square, so the cost is exponential and a single call can exceed the arena on its
own. Refusing with the reason beats dying inside an allocation.
