WHICH FIELD IS THE d=2048 SIC MODULI FIELD — SETTLED

The question was which of two ray class fields the fiducial coordinates
generate, since both were being called the SIC moduli field:

  A  conductor 4096, both infinite places unramified
     [4096, 1024, 8, 2], order 2^26 over F, degree 2^27 over Q
  B  conductor 2048, both infinite places ramified
     [4096, 512, 8, 4, 2], order 2^27 over F, degree 2^28 over Q

Degree alone cannot decide it: at d=12, five different conductors give the
right degree over F. The field has to be identified, not its size.

METHOD. Settle the convention at d=12, where the moduli field is independently
known to be x^8 - 10x^6 + 23x^4 - 16x^2 + 1, and read its conductor off
directly rather than predicting it.

  the field is totally real, signature [8, 0]
  it is NOT Galois over Q — its Galois group has order 16 for degree 8 —
    but it IS abelian over Q(sqrt 13), which is what a ray class field requires
  it is abelian over neither of its other quadratic subfields, Q(sqrt 2) and
    Q(sqrt 26), so the base is forced

  rnfconductor gives, exactly:
    finite part   p_2^3 * p_3     norm 192
    infinite part [0, 0]          BOTH REAL PLACES UNRAMIFIED

  exponent at 2 is 3, and v2(12) + 1 = 3.

CONCLUSION. The conductor rule is confirmed against a known field, and the
convention is infinity UNRAMIFIED. The full modulus is

    p_2^(v2(d)+1)  times the odd part of d,  infinity unramified.

d = 2048 = 2^11 has no odd part, and 2 is inert in F since m = 5 mod 8, so
p_2 = (2) and the modulus is p_2^12 = (4096) with infinity unramified.

That is candidate A. It is the field the Lean already carries:
moduli_field_degree_over_F = 2^26 and moduli_field_degree_over_Q = 2^27 in
SIC_D2048_Moduli.lean are correct as written.

Candidate B is not the moduli field. Identifying it as such conflates the
degree over F with the degree over Q — B is 2^27 over F but 2^28 over Q — and
ramifies at infinity, which the d=12 conductor rules out. A moduli field is
generated by absolute values, which are real, so unramified at infinity is what
it should be; B is the right shape for the FULL fiducial field, where the
phases live and complex conjugation acts, but not for the moduli.

CONSEQUENCE. The reduced character set must be taken in A, not B. The two
groups differ in their second invariant, 1024 against 512, so the index-1024
subgroups are not the same characters. An L-function run against B computes the
wrong 1024 characters. The run against B was stopped; the run against A
continues.
