## Obstruction located
The ob3ect's FSPLIT-on-factor does not uniformly reduce: across real Euler
bricks the shared factor is sometimes odd (3,5,7,33), sometimes even
(4,8,12,20), often several pairs at once, so no single extracted factor carries
all four Diophantine constraints down together. This is the descent's open core
= the perfect-cuboid conjecture on this branch.

## Continuation
The loop persists. Each rung banked is a true lemma; the summit is the open
conjecture. A stronger ob3ect (larger model, richer --context, --entry
grounding) seeds the next attempt.
