# The Millennium problems as operator-valued measures — crystallized proof

Kernel-branch data and proof for the manuscript on the Millennium problems read
as operator-valued measures.

`promotions.sh` reproduces the promotion table against the hosted kernel: five
promotion words derived, each asserted at its crystal and its parity slot. Run
it from the repository root; exit 0 is a pass.

## Two levels, kept apart

The reproducer reports two measurements and never compares their addresses:

- **kernel** — `imasm derive <word>` runs the word as a program and reports the
  structural witness of its control flow.
- **design** — the ob3ect's grounded tuple reads the *description* against the
  twelve axes.

These are different measurements of different things. Their crystal addresses
are not comparable and are never set against each other here. What is comparable
is the parity slot, and that is what the table asserts.

A second numbering trap is guarded explicitly: the self-encoding type has the
address 16840174 in the kernel's plain mixed-radix numbering and 6734591 in the
boundary-cell numbering used by the crystal navigator. Same type, two numberings.
Mixing them is the error the guard exists to prevent.

## Result

Four promotion words close the parity to or': the Riemann positivity promotion,
the BSD and Hodge parity promotions, and the Collatz or'-closure. All four land
on the descent crystal 3444190.

The fifth is the same Collatz promotion asked for differently — as a named move
from nun to or' rather than as a structure. Its word still closes the parity to
or' at kernel level, but lands on the fork 16404190, which differs from the
descent crystal only at the ⊢ mark (0 against 3); and its design grounding gives
yew rather than or'. The two levels disagree for this object alone: the control
flow closes, the reading of the description does not.

The self-encoding type carries parity or' together with winding ℤ. The proved
control, Lee-Yang, carries or' with winding Z2. These are two different targets,
and any statement about distance has to say which one it measures against.
